**Bouncing Ball** is a pixel-art physics platformer about a little tumbler on a bouncy red ball. Hold A / D to lean and charge, press Space to jump, and every landing bounces like a real ball. Bounce through hills, spikes, seesaws and enemies, grab hearts, shields, jump boosts and a gun, then take down a rolling boss. Fall, respawn and bounce back, in three handmade levels or an Endless Run that gets harder the further you go.
